# How much is separation pay?

## English

**How much is separation pay?**

For redundancy or labour-saving devices: one month’s pay per year of service. For retrenchment, closure not due to serious losses, or disease: half a month’s pay per year of service. Either way, at least one month’s pay; six months or more counts as a full year.

## Tagalog

**Magkano ang separation pay?**

Sa redundancy o labor-saving device: isang buwang sahod kada taon ng serbisyo. Sa retrenchment, pagsasara na hindi dahil sa malaking lugi, o sakit: kalahating buwan kada taon. Alinman, hindi bababa sa isang buwang sahod; ang 6 na buwan pataas ay bilang isang buong taon.

## 中文

**遣散费是多少？**

因岗位冗余或引进节省人力设备：每服务一年发一个月工资。因裁员、非严重亏损的停业或疾病：每服务一年发半个月工资。无论哪种至少一个月工资；满 6 个月按一年计。

Source: Labor Code of the Philippines, Arts. 298 and 299
