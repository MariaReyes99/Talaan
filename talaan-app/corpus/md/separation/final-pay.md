# When must final pay be released?

## English

**When must final pay be released?**

Within 30 days from the date the employee leaves, unless a company policy or agreement is more favourable. Final pay includes unpaid salary, prorated 13th month pay, cash value of unused service incentive leave and any other amounts due.

## Tagalog

**Kailan dapat ibigay ang final pay?**

Sa loob ng 30 araw mula sa pag-alis ng empleyado, maliban kung mas mabuti ang patakaran o kasunduan ng kumpanya. Kasama sa final pay ang hindi pa nabayarang sahod, prorated na 13th month pay, cash ng hindi nagamit na service incentive leave, at iba pang dapat bayaran.

## 中文

**最终工资必须什么时候发放？**

自员工离职之日起 30 天内发放，公司政策或协议更优的除外。最终工资包括未付工资、按比例的第十三个月工资、未用服务奖励假折现及其他应付款项。

Source: DOLE Labor Advisory No. 06, Series of 2020
