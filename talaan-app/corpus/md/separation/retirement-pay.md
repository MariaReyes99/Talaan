# How much is retirement pay without a company plan?

## English

**How much is retirement pay without a company plan?**

An employee aged 60 to 65 with at least 5 years of service gets at least 22.5 days’ pay per year of service: 15 days’ salary, plus one twelfth of 13th month pay, plus 5 days of service incentive leave.

## Tagalog

**Magkano ang retirement pay kung walang company plan?**

Ang empleyadong 60 hanggang 65 taong gulang na may hindi bababa sa 5 taon ng serbisyo ay makakatanggap ng hindi bababa sa 22.5 araw na sahod kada taon ng serbisyo: 15 araw na sahod, one twelfth ng 13th month pay, at 5 araw ng service incentive leave.

## 中文

**没有公司退休计划时，退休金是多少？**

60 至 65 岁、服务满 5 年的员工，每服务一年至少获得 22.5 天工资：15 天工资，加第十三个月工资的十二分之一，再加 5 天服务奖励假。
