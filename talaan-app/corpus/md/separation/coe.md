# How fast must a certificate of employment be issued?

## English

**How fast must a certificate of employment be issued?**

Within 3 days from the employee’s request.

## Tagalog

**Gaano kabilis dapat ibigay ang certificate of employment?**

Sa loob ng 3 araw mula nang hilingin ng empleyado.

## 中文

**在职或离职证明要多快开出？**

员工提出申请后 3 天内。
