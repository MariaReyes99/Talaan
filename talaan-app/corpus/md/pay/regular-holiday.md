# How is a regular holiday paid?

## English

**How is a regular holiday paid?**

If not worked, the employee still gets 100% of the daily rate. If worked, at least 200% of the daily rate for the first 8 hours, plus 30% more per hour of overtime.

## Tagalog

**Paano binabayaran ang regular holiday?**

Kung hindi pumasok, 100% pa rin ng daily rate ang matatanggap. Kung pumasok, hindi bababa sa 200% ng daily rate sa unang 8 oras, at dagdag na 30% kada oras ng overtime.

## 中文

**法定节假日工资怎么算？**

不上班也照付 100% 日薪。上班的话，前 8 小时至少按日薪的 200% 支付，加班每小时再加 30%。

Source: Labor Code of the Philippines, Art. 94; DOLE holiday pay rules
