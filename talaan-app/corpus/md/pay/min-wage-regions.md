# What is the minimum wage outside Metro Manila?

## English

**What is the minimum wage outside Metro Manila?**

Each region has its own wage order. Highest daily non-agriculture rates as of 28 October 2026: Region IV-A ₱645 (IVA-23, from 26 October 2026), Region III ₱600, Region VII ₱582 Class A (ROVII-27, from 14 October 2026), Region VI ₱550, Region XI ₱540 (from 1 September 2026), CAR and Region I ₱505, Regions II and X ₱500. Rates vary by area and sector, so pick the client’s region in Talaan and confirm with the regional wage board.

## Tagalog

**Magkano ang minimum wage sa labas ng Metro Manila?**

May sariling wage order ang bawat rehiyon. Pinakamataas na arawang non-agriculture rate hanggang 28 Oktubre 2026: Region IV-A ₱645 (IVA-23, mula 26 Oktubre 2026), Region III ₱600, Region VII ₱582 Class A (ROVII-27, mula 14 Oktubre 2026), Region VI ₱550, Region XI ₱540 (mula 1 Setyembre 2026), CAR at Region I ₱505, Region II at X ₱500. Iba-iba ang rate ayon sa lugar at sektor, kaya piliin ang rehiyon ng client sa Talaan at kumpirmahin sa regional wage board.

## 中文

**马尼拉大都会以外的最低工资是多少？**

各地区有各自的工资令。截至 2026 年 10 月 28 日的非农业最高日薪：Region IV-A ₱645（IVA-23，自 2026 年 10 月 26 日起），Region III ₱600，Region VII A 类 ₱582（ROVII-27，自 2026 年 10 月 14 日起），Region VI ₱550，Region XI ₱540（自 2026 年 9 月 1 日起），CAR 和 Region I ₱505，Region II 和 X ₱500。工资因地区和行业而异，请在 Talaan 中选择客户所在地区，并向地区工资委员会确认。
