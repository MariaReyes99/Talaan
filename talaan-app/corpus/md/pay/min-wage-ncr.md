# What is the minimum wage in Metro Manila?

## English

**What is the minimum wage in Metro Manila?**

From 26 September 2026, Wage Order NCR-28 sets ₱755 a day for non-agriculture and ₱718 for agriculture, retail and service with 15 or fewer workers, and manufacturing with fewer than 10. Rates differ by region; check the regional wage board and update each client’s minimum wage in Talaan.

## Tagalog

**Magkano ang minimum wage sa Metro Manila?**

Mula 26 Setyembre 2026, sa Wage Order NCR-28: ₱755 kada araw sa non-agriculture at ₱718 sa agriculture, retail at service na may 15 o mas kaunting manggagawa, at manufacturing na may mas kaunti sa 10. Iba-iba ito bawat rehiyon; tingnan ang regional wage board at i-update ang minimum wage ng bawat client sa Talaan.

## 中文

**马尼拉大都会的最低工资是多少？**

自 2026 年 9 月 26 日起，NCR-28 号工资令规定：非农业每日 ₱755；农业、15 人及以下的零售和服务业、不足 10 人的制造业每日 ₱718。各地区标准不同，请核对地区工资委员会公告，并在 Talaan 中更新每个客户的最低工资。
