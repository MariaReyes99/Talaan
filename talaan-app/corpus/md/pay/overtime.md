# How is overtime paid?

## English

**How is overtime paid?**

Work beyond 8 hours on an ordinary working day is paid at least 25% more than the hourly rate. Overtime on a rest day or holiday is paid at least 30% more than the hourly rate for that day.

## Tagalog

**Paano binabayaran ang overtime?**

Ang trabaho na lampas 8 oras sa ordinaryong araw ay may dagdag na hindi bababa sa 25% ng hourly rate. Ang overtime sa rest day o holiday ay may dagdag na hindi bababa sa 30% ng hourly rate sa araw na iyon.

## 中文

**加班费怎么算？**

普通工作日超过 8 小时的部分，至少按时薪加 25% 支付。休息日或节假日的加班，至少按当天时薪加 30% 支付。

Source: Labor Code of the Philippines, Art. 87
