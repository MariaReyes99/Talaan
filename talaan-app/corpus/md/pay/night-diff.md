# How is night differential paid?

## English

**How is night differential paid?**

Each hour worked between 10pm and 6am earns at least 10% more than the regular hourly rate.

## Tagalog

**Paano binabayaran ang night differential?**

Bawat oras ng trabaho mula 10pm hanggang 6am ay may dagdag na hindi bababa sa 10% ng regular na hourly rate.

## 中文

**夜班津贴怎么算？**

晚上 10 点至早上 6 点之间每工作 1 小时，至少按正常时薪加 10% 支付。

Source: Labor Code of the Philippines, Art. 86
