# How is a special non-working day paid?

## English

**How is a special non-working day paid?**

No work, no pay, unless company policy or a contract says otherwise. If worked, the employee gets at least 130% of the daily rate for the first 8 hours, or 150% if it is also their rest day.

## Tagalog

**Paano binabayaran ang special non-working day?**

No work, no pay, maliban kung iba ang patakaran ng kumpanya o kontrata. Kung pumasok, hindi bababa sa 130% ng daily rate sa unang 8 oras, o 150% kung rest day din niya.

## 中文

**特别非工作日工资怎么算？**

不上班不付薪，除非公司政策或合同另有规定。上班的话，前 8 小时至少按日薪的 130% 支付；如果当天也是其休息日，则为 150%。
