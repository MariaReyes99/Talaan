# How do we turn a monthly salary into a daily rate?

## English

**How do we turn a monthly salary into a daily rate?**

Daily rate = monthly salary × 12 ÷ the number of paid days a year. Use 261 for a 5-day week and 313 for a 6-day week. The hourly rate is the daily rate ÷ 8.

## Tagalog

**Paano kunin ang daily rate mula sa buwanang sahod?**

Daily rate = buwanang sahod × 12 ÷ bilang ng bayad na araw kada taon. Gamitin ang 261 kung 5 araw kada linggo at 313 kung 6 na araw. Ang hourly rate ay daily rate ÷ 8.

## 中文

**月薪怎么换算成日薪？**

日薪 = 月薪 × 12 ÷ 全年计薪天数。每周 5 天用 261，每周 6 天用 313。时薪 = 日薪 ÷ 8。
