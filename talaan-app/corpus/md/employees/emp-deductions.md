# What are the deductions on my payslip?

## English

**What are the deductions on my payslip?**

SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG are your share of government contributions; your employer pays its own share on top. Withholding tax is income tax taken in advance. Loans appear only if you have an SSS or Pag-IBIG loan.

## Tagalog

**Ano ang mga kaltas sa payslip ko?**

Ang SSS, PhilHealth at Pag-IBIG ay bahagi mo sa kontribusyon sa gobyerno; may sariling bahagi rin ang employer mo. Ang withholding tax ay income tax na kinakaltas nang maaga. Lalabas lang ang loan kung may SSS o Pag-IBIG loan ka.

## 中文

**工资单上的扣款是什么？**

SSS、PhilHealth 和 Pag-IBIG 是你应缴的政府缴款部分，雇主另外缴纳其部分。预扣税是提前扣缴的个人所得税。只有在你有 SSS 或 Pag-IBIG 贷款时才会显示贷款扣款。
