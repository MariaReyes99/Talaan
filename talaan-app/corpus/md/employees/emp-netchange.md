# Why is my net pay different this cutoff?

## English

**Why is my net pay different this cutoff?**

Net pay changes with absences, overtime, night hours, holidays worked, allowances and loan deductions. Open the payslip to see each line. If something looks wrong, ask your HR or payroll contact.

## Tagalog

**Bakit iba ang net pay ko ngayong cutoff?**

Nagbabago ang net pay dahil sa absent, overtime, oras sa gabi, holiday na pumasok, allowance at kaltas sa loan. Buksan ang payslip para makita ang bawat linya. Kung may mukhang mali, magtanong sa HR o payroll.

## 中文

**为什么这一期的实发工资不一样？**

实发工资会随缺勤、加班、夜班、节假日上班、津贴和贷款扣款而变化。打开工资单可查看每一项。如有疑问，请联系人事或工资负责人。
