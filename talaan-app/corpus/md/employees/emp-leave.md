# How do I request leave and see how many days I have left?

## English

**How do I request leave and see how many days I have left?**

Open My leave. Your balances for this year are at the top. Press Request leave, choose the type and dates, and send. Your employer approves it and you get a notice. Leave without pay is deducted from that cutoff’s pay.

## Tagalog

**Paano humiling ng leave at makita ang natitirang araw?**

Buksan ang Aking leave. Nasa itaas ang balanse mo ngayong taon. Pindutin ang Humiling ng leave, piliin ang uri at petsa, at ipadala. Aaprubahan ito ng employer mo at makakatanggap ka ng abiso. Ibinabawas sa sahod ng cutoff na iyon ang leave na walang bayad.

## 中文

**怎么申请请假、查看剩余天数？**

打开“我的假期”，顶部显示本年余额。点击“申请请假”，选择类型和日期后提交。雇主审批后你会收到通知。无薪假会从当期工资中扣除。
