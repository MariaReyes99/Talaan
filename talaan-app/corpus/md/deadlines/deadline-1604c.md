# When are Form 1604-C and the alphalist due?

## English

**When are Form 1604-C and the alphalist due?**

By January 31 of the following year, covering all compensation and tax withheld in the year. The totals must match the monthly 1601-C returns and each employee’s Form 2316.

## Tagalog

**Kailan ang deadline ng Form 1604-C at alphalist?**

Hanggang January 31 ng susunod na taon, para sa lahat ng sahod at tax na nakaltas sa taon. Dapat tugma ang mga total sa buwanang 1601-C at sa Form 2316 ng bawat empleyado.

## 中文

**1604-C 表格和员工名册何时截止？**

次年 1 月 31 日前，涵盖全年所有工资和预扣税。合计数必须与每月 1601-C 申报及每位员工的 2316 表格一致。
