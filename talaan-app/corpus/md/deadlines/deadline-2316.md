# When is BIR Form 2316 due?

## English

**When is BIR Form 2316 due?**

Employers give each employee a signed Form 2316 by January 31, including employees who left during the year. Copies for substituted filing are submitted to the BIR by February 28.

## Tagalog

**Kailan ang deadline ng BIR Form 2316?**

Ibibigay ng employer ang pirmadong Form 2316 sa bawat empleyado hanggang January 31, kasama ang mga umalis sa taon. Ang kopya para sa substituted filing ay isusumite sa BIR hanggang February 28.

## 中文

**BIR 2316 表格何时截止？**

雇主须在 1 月 31 日前向每位员工（包括年内离职员工）发放已签署的 2316 表格；替代申报的副本须在 2 月 28 日前提交 BIR。

Source: BIR filing calendar; RR 11-2018
