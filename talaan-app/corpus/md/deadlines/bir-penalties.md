# What happens if a BIR return is filed late?

## English

**What happens if a BIR return is filed late?**

Late filing or payment generally costs a 25% surcharge plus 12% interest a year. Under the Ease of Paying Taxes Act, micro and small taxpayers pay a reduced 10% surcharge and half the interest. A compromise penalty may also apply.

## Tagalog

**Ano ang mangyayari kung late ang pag-file sa BIR?**

Karaniwan, 25% surcharge at 12% interest kada taon. Sa ilalim ng Ease of Paying Taxes Act, ang micro at small taxpayer ay 10% surcharge lang at kalahati ng interest. Puwede ring may compromise penalty.

## 中文

**BIR 逾期申报会怎样？**

逾期申报或缴税一般加收 25% 附加费，另按年利率 12% 计息。根据《便利纳税法》，微型和小型纳税人的附加费降为 10%，利息减半。还可能有和解罚款。

Source: NIRC Secs. 248 and 249, as amended by RA 11976 (Ease of Paying Taxes Act)
