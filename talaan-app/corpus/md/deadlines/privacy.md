# What privacy rules apply to payroll data?

## English

**What privacy rules apply to payroll data?**

Government ID numbers, health and salary details are personal information, and ID numbers and health data are sensitive. Limit access to people who need it, keep records secure, and report a serious breach to the National Privacy Commission within 72 hours.

## Tagalog

**Anong patakaran sa privacy ang para sa payroll data?**

Ang government ID number, health at sahod ay personal information, at sensitive ang ID number at health data. Limitahan ang access sa mga kailangan lang, panatilihing ligtas, at i-report ang seryosong breach sa National Privacy Commission sa loob ng 72 oras.

## 中文

**工资数据适用哪些隐私规定？**

政府证件号码、健康和薪资信息都属于个人信息，其中证件号码和健康数据属于敏感信息。仅限有需要的人访问，妥善保管，发生严重泄露须在 72 小时内向国家隐私委员会报告。

Source: RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012); NPC Circular 16-03
