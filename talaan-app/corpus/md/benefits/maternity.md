# How long is maternity leave?

## English

**How long is maternity leave?**

105 days with full pay for live childbirth, plus 15 days more for solo mothers, and 60 days for miscarriage or emergency termination of pregnancy. The mother may extend by 30 days without pay and may give up to 7 of her days to the child’s father.

## Tagalog

**Gaano katagal ang maternity leave?**

105 araw na may buong sahod sa panganganak, dagdag na 15 araw sa solo mother, at 60 araw kung nakunan. Puwedeng i-extend ng 30 araw nang walang bayad, at puwedeng ibigay ang hanggang 7 araw sa ama ng bata.

## 中文

**产假有多长？**

正常分娩享有 105 天全薪产假，单亲母亲再加 15 天，流产或紧急终止妊娠为 60 天。可再延长 30 天无薪假，并可把最多 7 天转给孩子的父亲。
