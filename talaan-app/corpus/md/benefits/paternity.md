# How long is paternity leave?

## English

**How long is paternity leave?**

7 days with full pay for a married father, for the first four deliveries of his lawful wife. He can get up to 7 more days if the mother transfers part of her maternity leave. A bill to extend paternity leave was filed in 2026 but is not law.

## Tagalog

**Gaano katagal ang paternity leave?**

7 araw na may buong sahod para sa amang may asawa, para sa unang apat na panganganak ng kanyang asawa. Puwedeng madagdagan ng hanggang 7 araw kung ilipat ng ina ang bahagi ng kanyang maternity leave. May panukalang batas noong 2026 para pahabain ito pero hindi pa batas.

## 中文

**陪产假有多长？**

已婚父亲在合法配偶前四次分娩时享有 7 天全薪陪产假。如母亲转让部分产假，可再增加最多 7 天。2026 年已有延长陪产假的议案，但尚未成为法律。

Source: RA 8187 (Paternity Leave Act of 1996); RA 11210 (transferred days)
