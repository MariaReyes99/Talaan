# What leave do solo parents get?

## English

**What leave do solo parents get?**

Solo parents with at least 6 months of service get up to 7 working days of paid parental leave a year, on top of other leave.

## Tagalog

**Anong leave ang para sa solo parent?**

Ang solo parent na may hindi bababa sa 6 na buwan ng serbisyo ay may hanggang 7 araw ng trabaho na bayad na parental leave kada taon, dagdag sa ibang leave.

## 中文

**单亲父母有什么假？**

工作满 6 个月的单亲父母每年享有最多 7 个工作日的带薪育儿假，另计于其他假期。

Source: RA 11861 (Expanded Solo Parents Welfare Act)
