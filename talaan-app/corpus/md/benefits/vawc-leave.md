# What is VAWC leave?

## English

**What is VAWC leave?**

A woman employee who is a victim of violence under RA 9262 can take up to 10 days of paid leave, extendable when needed, to attend to medical and legal concerns.

## Tagalog

**Ano ang VAWC leave?**

Ang babaeng empleyado na biktima ng karahasan sa ilalim ng RA 9262 ay may hanggang 10 araw na bayad na leave, na puwedeng pahabain kung kailangan, para sa medikal at legal na usapin.

## 中文

**什么是 VAWC 假？**

根据 RA 9262 遭受暴力的女性员工，可享有最多 10 天带薪假（必要时可延长），用于处理医疗和法律事务。

Source: RA 9262 (Anti-Violence Against Women and Their Children Act), Sec. 43
