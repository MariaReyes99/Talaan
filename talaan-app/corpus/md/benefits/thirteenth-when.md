# How is 13th month pay computed and when is it due?

## English

**How is 13th month pay computed and when is it due?**

Rank-and-file employees get at least one twelfth of the basic salary they earned during the year, paid by December 24. Employees who leave during the year get a prorated amount with their final pay.

## Tagalog

**Paano kinukuwenta ang 13th month pay at kailan ito ibinibigay?**

Ang rank-and-file ay makakatanggap ng hindi bababa sa one twelfth ng basic salary na kinita sa taon, ibibigay hanggang December 24. Ang umalis sa kalagitnaan ng taon ay may prorated na halaga kasama ng final pay.

## 中文

**第十三个月工资怎么算？何时发放？**

普通员工至少获得当年所得基本工资的十二分之一，最迟 12 月 24 日发放。年中离职的员工按比例计算，随最终工资一并发放。
