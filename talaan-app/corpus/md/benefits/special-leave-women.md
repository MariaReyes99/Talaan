# What is the special leave for women?

## English

**What is the special leave for women?**

A woman employee with at least 6 months of service in the last 12 months can take up to 2 months of leave with full pay after surgery for a gynecological disorder.

## Tagalog

**Ano ang special leave para sa kababaihan?**

Ang babaeng empleyado na may hindi bababa sa 6 na buwang serbisyo sa nakaraang 12 buwan ay puwedeng mag-leave nang hanggang 2 buwan na may buong sahod pagkatapos ng operasyon sa gynecological na kondisyon.

## 中文

**女性特别假是什么？**

过去 12 个月内工作满 6 个月的女性员工，因妇科疾病手术后可享有最多 2 个月全薪假。

Source: RA 9710 (Magna Carta of Women), Sec. 18
