# What is service incentive leave?

## English

**What is service incentive leave?**

Employees with at least one year of service get 5 days of paid leave a year. Unused days are converted to cash at the end of the year or when the employee leaves. Some employers, such as those with fewer than 10 workers, are exempt.

## Tagalog

**Ano ang service incentive leave?**

Ang empleyadong may hindi bababa sa isang taon ng serbisyo ay may 5 araw na bayad na leave kada taon. Ang hindi nagamit ay ginagawang cash sa katapusan ng taon o pag-alis ng empleyado. May ilang exempted, gaya ng employer na may mas kaunti sa 10 manggagawa.

## 中文

**什么是服务奖励假？**

工作满一年的员工每年享有 5 天带薪假。未用完的天数在年底或离职时折算成现金。部分雇主可豁免，例如员工不足 10 人的雇主。

Source: Labor Code of the Philippines, Art. 95
