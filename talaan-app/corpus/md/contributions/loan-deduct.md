# Must the employer deduct SSS and Pag-IBIG loan payments?

## English

**Must the employer deduct SSS and Pag-IBIG loan payments?**

Yes. When SSS or Pag-IBIG bills a member loan, the employer deducts the monthly amortization from pay and remits it with the contributions (SSS ML-2, Pag-IBIG STLRF). Late remittance carries penalties. Record each loan in Talaan so the deduction and balance are tracked.

## Tagalog

**Kailangan bang kaltasan ng employer ang bayad sa SSS at Pag-IBIG loan?**

Oo. Kapag nag-bill ang SSS o Pag-IBIG ng loan ng miyembro, kinakaltas ng employer ang buwanang hulog sa sahod at nire-remit kasama ng kontribusyon (SSS ML-2, Pag-IBIG STLRF). May multa ang late na remittance. Itala ang bawat loan sa Talaan para masubaybayan ang kaltas at balanse.

## 中文

**雇主必须代扣 SSS 和 Pag-IBIG 贷款还款吗？**

是的。SSS 或 Pag-IBIG 对会员贷款出账后，雇主须从工资中扣除每月还款，并随缴款一起上缴（SSS ML-2、Pag-IBIG STLRF）。逾期上缴会被罚款。请在 Talaan 中登记每笔贷款，以便跟踪扣款和余额。
