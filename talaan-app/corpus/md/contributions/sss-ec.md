# What is the EC contribution and who pays it?

## English

**What is the EC contribution and who pays it?**

The Employees’ Compensation (EC) contribution is paid by the employer only: ₱10 a month when the salary credit is below ₱15,000, and ₱30 a month from ₱15,000 up. It is not deducted from the employee.

## Tagalog

**Ano ang EC contribution at sino ang nagbabayad?**

Ang Employees’ Compensation (EC) ay employer lang ang nagbabayad: ₱10 kada buwan kung mababa sa ₱15,000 ang salary credit, at ₱30 kung ₱15,000 pataas. Hindi ito kinakaltas sa empleyado.

## 中文

**EC 缴款是什么？由谁缴纳？**

雇员补偿（EC）缴款只由雇主缴纳：月薪基数低于 ₱15,000 时每月 ₱10，₱15,000 及以上每月 ₱30。不从员工工资中扣除。
