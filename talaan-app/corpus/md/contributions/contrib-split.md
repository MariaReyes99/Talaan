# How are monthly contributions handled when we pay twice a month?

## English

**How are monthly contributions handled when we pay twice a month?**

Agencies compute SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG per month. Talaan splits each monthly amount evenly across the two cutoffs; some employers deduct it all in one cutoff instead. Either is fine as long as the monthly total is right.

## Tagalog

**Paano ang buwanang kontribusyon kung dalawang beses magpasahod?**

Buwanan kinukuwenta ng mga ahensya ang SSS, PhilHealth at Pag-IBIG. Hinahati ito ng Talaan nang pantay sa dalawang cutoff; ang ibang employer ay kinakaltas lahat sa isang cutoff. Ayos lang alinman basta tama ang total kada buwan.

## 中文

**每月发薪两次时，月度缴款怎么处理？**

各机构按月计算 SSS、PhilHealth 和 Pag-IBIG。Talaan 把月度金额平均分到两个周期；也有雇主在其中一期一次扣完。只要每月合计正确，两种做法都可以。
