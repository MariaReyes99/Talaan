# How much is the PhilHealth premium?

## English

**How much is the PhilHealth premium?**

PhilHealth premiums are 5% of basic monthly salary, shared equally by employer and employee. Salaries below ₱10,000 are charged as ₱10,000, and salaries above ₱100,000 as ₱100,000.

## Tagalog

**Magkano ang PhilHealth premium?**

Ang PhilHealth premium ay 5% ng basic monthly salary, hati ang employer at empleyado. Kung mababa sa ₱10,000 ang sahod, ₱10,000 ang batayan; kung lampas ₱100,000, ₱100,000 ang batayan.

## 中文

**PhilHealth 保费是多少？**

PhilHealth 保费为月基本工资的 5%，雇主和员工各付一半。月薪低于 ₱10,000 按 ₱10,000 计，高于 ₱100,000 按 ₱100,000 计。
