# How much is the SSS contribution?

## English

**How much is the SSS contribution?**

SSS contributions are 15% of the monthly salary credit: 10% paid by the employer and 5% by the employee. The salary credit runs from ₱5,000 to ₱35,000, in ₱500 steps.

## Tagalog

**Magkano ang SSS contribution?**

Ang SSS contribution ay 15% ng monthly salary credit: 10% ang employer at 5% ang empleyado. Ang salary credit ay mula ₱5,000 hanggang ₱35,000, kada ₱500.

## 中文

**SSS 缴款是多少？**

SSS 缴款为月薪基数的 15%：雇主 10%，员工 5%。月薪基数从 ₱5,000 到 ₱35,000，每档 ₱500。
