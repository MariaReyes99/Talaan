# How much is the Pag-IBIG contribution?

## English

**How much is the Pag-IBIG contribution?**

The employee pays 2% (1% if earning ₱1,500 or less) and the employer pays 2%, on pay up to ₱10,000. That is at most ₱200 each a month.

## Tagalog

**Magkano ang Pag-IBIG contribution?**

Ang empleyado ay 2% (1% kung kumikita ng ₱1,500 o mas mababa) at ang employer ay 2%, sa sahod hanggang ₱10,000. Hanggang ₱200 bawat isa kada buwan.

## 中文

**Pag-IBIG 缴款是多少？**

员工缴 2%（月收入 ₱1,500 或以下为 1%），雇主缴 2%，计费工资上限 ₱10,000，即每人每月最多 ₱200。

Source: HDMF Circular No. 460 (2024)
