# What is the penalty for paying SSS late?

## English

**What is the penalty for paying SSS late?**

Late SSS contributions carry a penalty of 2% a month on the unpaid amount until paid. Unremitted employee deductions can also lead to criminal liability.

## Tagalog

**Ano ang multa kapag late ang bayad sa SSS?**

May penalty na 2% kada buwan sa hindi nabayarang SSS contribution hanggang mabayaran. Puwede ring magkaroon ng kasong kriminal kapag hindi na-remit ang kinaltas sa empleyado.

## 中文

**SSS 逾期缴款有什么处罚？**

逾期的 SSS 缴款按未缴金额每月加收 2% 罚款，直至缴清。已扣员工款项而未上缴的，还可能承担刑事责任。

Source: RA 11199, Sec. 22 (penalty on late remittance)
