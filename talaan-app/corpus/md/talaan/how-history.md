# How do I see who changed an employee’s salary?

## English

**How do I see who changed an employee’s salary?**

Open the client, then the 🕘 button next to the employee, or HR → People. Every change to salary, position, bank account or ID numbers is recorded with who made it, when and the reason given. Bank and ID numbers are masked in the history.

## Tagalog

**Paano makita kung sino ang nagpalit ng sahod ng empleyado?**

Buksan ang client, saka ang 🕘 na button sa tabi ng empleyado, o ang HR → Mga tao. Naitatala ang bawat pagbabago sa sahod, posisyon, bank account o ID number kasama kung sino, kailan at ang dahilan. Nakatago ang bank at ID number sa kasaysayan.

## 中文

**怎么查看谁修改了员工工资？**

打开客户，点击员工旁的 🕘 按钮，或进入“人事 → 员工”。工资、职位、银行账号或证件号码的每次修改都会记录操作人、时间和原因。银行账号和证件号码在记录中会被遮盖。
