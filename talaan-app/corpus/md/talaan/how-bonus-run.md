# How do I pay the 13th month or a bonus separately?

## English

**How do I pay the 13th month or a bonus separately?**

In Payroll → Pay runs, press Bonus or reimbursement run. Choose the client, the cutoff to pay with and the employees. For the 13th month, Talaan fills in basic pay earned this year divided by 12, less what was already paid, and taxes only the part above the ₱90,000 limit. No SSS, PhilHealth or Pag-IBIG is deducted. It goes through the same checks and approval.

## Tagalog

**Paano ibigay nang hiwalay ang 13th month o bonus?**

Sa Payroll → Mga pay run, pindutin ang Pay run para sa bonus o reimbursement. Piliin ang client, cutoff na kasabay ng bayad at mga empleyado. Para sa 13th month, pupunan ng Talaan ang basic pay ngayong taon na hinati sa 12, bawas ang naibigay na, at ang lampas lang sa ₱90,000 na limit ang bubuwisan. Walang kaltas na SSS, PhilHealth o Pag-IBIG. Dadaan ito sa parehong check at approval.

## 中文

**怎么单独发放第13个月工资或奖金？**

在“工资 → 发薪批次”中点击“奖金或报销发薪”，选择客户、随哪一期发放以及员工。第13个月工资由 Talaan 按本年基本工资除以 12、减去已发金额自动填写，只对超过 ₱90,000 限额的部分计税，不扣 SSS、PhilHealth 或 Pag-IBIG。同样需要检查和审批。
