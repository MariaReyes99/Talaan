# How do I deduct an SSS loan or a cash advance?

## English

**How do I deduct an SSS loan or a cash advance?**

Go to Payroll → Loans and deductions and press Add loan. Enter the amount borrowed, the deduction per cutoff and the first cutoff. Talaan deducts it in every regular pay run, shows the balance and stops at zero. SSS and Pag-IBIG loan amounts must match the agency’s billing.

## Tagalog

**Paano kaltasan ang SSS loan o cash advance?**

Pumunta sa Payroll → Loan at kaltas at pindutin ang Magdagdag ng loan. Ilagay ang halagang hiniram, kaltas bawat cutoff at unang cutoff. Kakaltasin ito ng Talaan sa bawat regular na pay run, ipapakita ang balanse at hihinto kapag zero na. Dapat tugma ang halaga ng SSS at Pag-IBIG loan sa billing ng ahensya.

## 中文

**怎么扣 SSS 贷款或预支款？**

进入“工资 → 借款与扣款”，点击“添加借款”，填写借款金额、每期扣款和首次扣款期。Talaan 会在每次常规发薪时扣除，显示余额，余额为零时停止。SSS 和 Pag-IBIG 贷款金额必须与机构账单一致。
