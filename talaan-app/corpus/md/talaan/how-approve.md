# Why can’t I approve the pay run?

## English

**Why can’t I approve the pay run?**

The person who prepared a pay run cannot approve it. The client owner or the firm owner must approve. After approval the pay run is locked, so changes go into the next pay run as adjustments.

## Tagalog

**Bakit hindi ko ma-approve ang pay run?**

Hindi puwedeng mag-approve ang naghanda ng pay run. Ang may-ari ng client o ng firm ang dapat mag-approve. Pagkatapos ma-approve, naka-lock na ito, kaya ang mga pagbabago ay ilalagay sa susunod na pay run bilang adjustment.

## 中文

**为什么我不能批准发薪？**

准备发薪的人不能自己批准，须由客户老板或事务所负责人批准。批准后发薪即被锁定，如需修改，请在下一次发薪中作为调整处理。
