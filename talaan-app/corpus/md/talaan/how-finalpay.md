# How do I process final pay in Talaan?

## English

**How do I process final pay in Talaan?**

In Payroll → Pay runs, press Final pay, choose the employee, the last day of work and the reason. Talaan works out the last salary, the pro-rated 13th month, unused convertible leave and the tax adjustment for the year, and prepares a certificate of employment. Release it within 30 days unless a better company policy applies.

## Tagalog

**Paano mag-process ng final pay sa Talaan?**

Sa Payroll → Mga pay run, pindutin ang Final pay, piliin ang empleyado, huling araw ng trabaho at dahilan. Kukuwentahin ng Talaan ang huling sahod, pro-rated na 13th month, hindi nagamit na leave na puwedeng gawing pera at adjustment sa buwis ngayong taon, at ihahanda ang certificate of employment. Ibigay ito sa loob ng 30 araw maliban kung may mas magandang patakaran ang kumpanya.

## 中文

**在 Talaan 中怎么办理离职结算？**

在“工资 → 发薪批次”中点击“离职结算”，选择员工、最后工作日和原因。Talaan 计算最后工资、按比例的第13个月工资、可折现的未休假期和本年度税款调整，并准备在职证明。除非公司政策更优惠，应在 30 天内发放。
