# How do I track what clients owe and record their payments?

## English

**How do I track what clients owe and record their payments?**

Billing → Invoices shows Receivables at the top: outstanding and past-due balances by age (not yet due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and over 90 days) and days to collect. Payments made online are recorded automatically. Proofs that clients upload wait under Payments to confirm: open the file, check your bank or e-wallet statement, then confirm or reject with a reason. Someone else must confirm a payment you sent. Use Record payment for money received directly, including any amount withheld on BIR Form 2307, and Statement of account for each client’s running balance.

## Tagalog

**Paano subaybayan ang utang ng mga client at itala ang bayad nila?**

Ipinapakita ng Billing → Mga invoice ang Mga receivable sa itaas: hindi pa bayad at lampas na sa due date ayon sa tagal (hindi pa due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 at higit 90 araw) at ilang araw bago makolekta. Kusang naitatala ang bayad online. Naghihintay sa Mga bayad na kukumpirmahin ang patunay na in-upload ng client: buksan ang file, suriin ang bank o e-wallet statement, saka kumpirmahin o tanggihan nang may dahilan. Ibang tao ang dapat magkumpirma ng bayad na ikaw ang nagpadala. Gamitin ang Itala ang bayad para sa perang direktang natanggap, kasama ang kinaltas sa BIR Form 2307, at ang Statement of account para sa tumatakbong balanse ng bawat client.

## 中文

**怎么跟踪客户欠款并记录收款？**

“账单 → 发票”顶部显示应收账款：按账龄（未到期、1–30、31–60、61–90 和 90 天以上）列出的未收和逾期余额，以及平均收款天数。在线付款会自动入账。客户上传的凭证会在“待确认付款”中等待：打开文件，核对银行或电子钱包流水后确认，或注明原因拒绝。您提交的付款须由他人确认。直接收到的款项（包括按 BIR 2307 表代扣的金额）请用“记录收款”，“对账单”显示每个客户的累计余额。
