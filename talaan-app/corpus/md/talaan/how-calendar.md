# Where can I see all the deadlines for my clients?

## English

**Where can I see all the deadlines for my clients?**

Compliance → Deadline calendar shows every client’s paydays and SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG and BIR due dates in one month. Green is done, amber is due within 3 days and red is late. Export it to Google Calendar or Outlook as a CSV.

## Tagalog

**Saan makikita ang lahat ng deadline ng mga client ko?**

Ipinapakita ng Compliance → Kalendaryo ng deadline ang sahuran at due date ng SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG at BIR ng bawat client sa isang buwan. Berde ang tapos, dilaw kung due sa loob ng 3 araw at pula kung huli. I-export ito sa Google Calendar o Outlook bilang CSV.

## 中文

**在哪里查看所有客户的截止日？**

“合规 → 截止日日历”在一个月视图中显示所有客户的发薪日以及 SSS、PhilHealth、Pag-IBIG 和 BIR 到期日。绿色为已完成，黄色为 3 天内到期，红色为逾期。可导出为 CSV，导入 Google 日历或 Outlook。
