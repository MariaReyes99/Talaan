# How do I set up recurring allowances and deductions?

## English

**How do I set up recurring allowances and deductions?**

Add the item once under Payroll → Pay items (taxable allowance, de minimis, deduction or reimbursement). Then open Payroll → Pay templates, choose the client and add the item and amount per cutoff for each employee. New regular pay runs fill them in automatically; open a draft and press Refresh to update it.

## Tagalog

**Paano mag-set up ng umuulit na allowance at kaltas?**

Idagdag ang item nang isang beses sa Payroll → Mga pay item (allowance na may buwis, de minimis, kaltas o reimbursement). Saka buksan ang Payroll → Mga pay template, piliin ang client at idagdag ang item at halaga bawat cutoff para sa bawat empleyado. Kusang pupunan ito ng mga bagong regular na pay run; buksan ang draft at pindutin ang Refresh para i-update.

## 中文

**怎么设置每期固定的津贴和扣款？**

先在“工资 → 工资项目”中添加一次（应税津贴、小额免税福利、扣款或报销）。然后打开“工资 → 工资模板”，选择客户，为每名员工添加项目和每期金额。新的常规发薪会自动填入；打开草稿并点击“刷新”即可更新。
