# Where are the dashboards and reports?

## English

**Where are the dashboards and reports?**

Analytics → Dashboard shows employees, payroll cost, government payments, on-time filing and cost by department, for all clients or one. Analytics → Reports downloads the payroll summary, year to date by employee, remittances, cost by department, leave balances and loans as CSV for Excel.

## Tagalog

**Nasaan ang dashboard at mga report?**

Ipinapakita ng Analytics → Dashboard ang empleyado, gastos sa payroll, bayad sa gobyerno, filing sa oras at gastos ayon sa department, para sa lahat ng client o isa. Dina-download ng Analytics → Mga report ang buod ng payroll, year to date bawat empleyado, remittance, gastos ayon sa department, balanse ng leave at loan bilang CSV para sa Excel.

## 中文

**看板和报表在哪里？**

“分析 → 看板”显示全部或单个客户的员工人数、工资成本、政府缴款、按时申报和各部门成本。“分析 → 报表”可将工资汇总、员工年初至今、缴款、各部门成本、假期余额和借款下载为 CSV，用 Excel 打开。
