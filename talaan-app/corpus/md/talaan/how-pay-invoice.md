# How do I pay my accounting firm’s invoice in Talaan?

## English

**How do I pay my accounting firm’s invoice in Talaan?**

Open More → Invoices and press Pay now on the invoice. Paying online with GCash, Maya, card or online banking is quickest: the invoice updates straight away and a receipt is emailed. If you paid another way, such as a bank deposit or cheque, press Paid another way, attach the deposit slip or transfer confirmation and send it. Your firm checks it against its statement and confirms or tells you why not.

## Tagalog

**Paano bayaran ang invoice ng accounting firm ko sa Talaan?**

Buksan ang Higit pa → Mga invoice at pindutin ang Magbayad ngayon sa invoice. Pinakamabilis ang online na bayad gamit ang GCash, Maya, card o online banking: agad na-a-update ang invoice at may resibo sa email. Kung nagbayad ka sa ibang paraan, gaya ng deposito o tseke, pindutin ang Nagbayad sa ibang paraan, ilakip ang deposit slip o kumpirmasyon ng transfer at ipadala. Susuriin ito ng firm mo laban sa statement at kukumpirmahin o sasabihin kung bakit hindi.

## 中文

**怎么在 Talaan 中支付会计事务所的发票？**

打开“更多 → 发票”，在发票上点击“立即付款”。使用 GCash、Maya、银行卡或网上银行在线支付最快：发票立即更新，并通过邮件发送收据。如果您已通过其他方式付款（如银行存款或支票），点击“已通过其他方式付款”，附上存款单或转账确认后提交。事务所会与流水核对后确认，或告知不接受的原因。
