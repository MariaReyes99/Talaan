# Which withholding tax table do we use?

## English

**Which withholding tax table do we use?**

Use the TRAIN law table in effect from 2023. Semi-monthly: zero up to ₱10,417; 15% of the excess over ₱10,417 up to ₱16,667; ₱937.50 plus 20% over ₱16,667 up to ₱33,333; and higher brackets above that. Taxable pay is gross pay minus non-taxable items and the employee’s SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG.

## Tagalog

**Anong withholding tax table ang gagamitin?**

Gamitin ang TRAIN law table mula 2023. Semi-monthly: walang tax hanggang ₱10,417; 15% ng sobra sa ₱10,417 hanggang ₱16,667; ₱937.50 at 20% ng sobra sa ₱16,667 hanggang ₱33,333; at mas mataas pa lampas doon. Ang taxable ay gross pay bawas ang non-taxable at ang SSS, PhilHealth at Pag-IBIG ng empleyado.

## 中文

**预扣税用哪张税表？**

使用 2023 年起生效的 TRAIN 法税表。半月薪：₱10,417 以下免税；₱10,417 至 ₱16,667 之间按超出部分的 15%；₱16,667 至 ₱33,333 之间为 ₱937.50 加超出部分的 20%；更高档次依此类推。应税工资为应发工资减去免税项目及员工承担的 SSS、PhilHealth 和 Pag-IBIG。
