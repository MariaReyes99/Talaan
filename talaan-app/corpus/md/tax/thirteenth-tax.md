# Is 13th month pay taxable?

## English

**Is 13th month pay taxable?**

13th month pay and other benefits are tax-free up to ₱90,000 a year in total. Only the amount above ₱90,000 is taxable.

## Tagalog

**May tax ba ang 13th month pay?**

Walang tax ang 13th month pay at iba pang benefits hanggang ₱90,000 kada taon. Ang sobra lang sa ₱90,000 ang may tax.

## 中文

**第十三个月工资要缴税吗？**

第十三个月工资及其他福利每年合计 ₱90,000 以内免税，只有超过 ₱90,000 的部分需要缴税。

Source: NIRC Sec. 32(B)(7)(e), as amended by RA 10963 (TRAIN Law)
