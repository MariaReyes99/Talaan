# What are the 2026 de minimis limits?

## English

**What are the 2026 de minimis limits?**

From 6 January 2026: rice subsidy ₱2,500 a month; uniform and clothing ₱8,000 a year; medical cash allowance to dependents ₱2,000 a semester; laundry ₱400 a month; Christmas and anniversary gifts ₱6,000 a year; achievement awards and CBA or productivity incentives ₱12,000 a year; overtime meal allowance 30% of the regional minimum wage. Amounts above a limit move into the ₱90,000 other-benefits ceiling.

## Tagalog

**Ano ang mga limit ng de minimis sa 2026?**

Mula 6 Enero 2026: rice subsidy ₱2,500 kada buwan; uniform at damit ₱8,000 kada taon; medical cash allowance sa dependents ₱2,000 kada semester; laundry ₱400 kada buwan; regalo sa Pasko at anibersaryo ₱6,000 kada taon; achievement awards at CBA o productivity incentive ₱12,000 kada taon; meal allowance sa overtime 30% ng regional minimum wage. Ang sobra ay papasok sa ₱90,000 na ceiling ng iba pang benefits.

## 中文

**2026 年小额免税福利的上限是多少？**

自 2026 年 1 月 6 日起：大米补贴每月 ₱2,500；制服和服装每年 ₱8,000；家属医疗现金津贴每半年 ₱2,000；洗衣津贴每月 ₱400；圣诞及周年礼品每年 ₱6,000；成就奖及集体协议或生产力奖励每年 ₱12,000；加班餐补为地区最低工资的 30%。超出上限的部分计入 ₱90,000 其他福利额度。
