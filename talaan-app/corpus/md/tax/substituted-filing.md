# Do employees need to file their own income tax return?

## English

**Do employees need to file their own income tax return?**

Not if they qualify for substituted filing: one employer for the whole year, compensation income only, and tax withheld correctly. The employer’s Form 2316 filed with the BIR takes the place of their return.

## Tagalog

**Kailangan bang mag-file ng sariling ITR ang empleyado?**

Hindi, kung qualified sa substituted filing: iisang employer sa buong taon, sahod lang ang kita, at tama ang nakaltas na tax. Ang Form 2316 na isinumite ng employer sa BIR ang papalit sa kanilang ITR.

## 中文

**员工需要自己报个人所得税吗？**

符合“替代申报”条件的不需要：全年只有一个雇主、只有工资收入、且税款已正确预扣。雇主向 BIR 提交的 2316 表格即视为其纳税申报。
