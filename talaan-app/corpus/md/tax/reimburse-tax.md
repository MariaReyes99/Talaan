# Are reimbursements to employees taxable?

## English

**Are reimbursements to employees taxable?**

Expenses an employee pays for the business, such as fuel, meals or training, are not compensation when they are reimbursed against receipts in the employer’s name and liquidated. They are added to net pay and not taxed. Fixed allowances paid without receipts are taxable unless they are de minimis.

## Tagalog

**May buwis ba ang reimbursement sa empleyado?**

Hindi sahod ang gastos na binayaran ng empleyado para sa negosyo, gaya ng fuel, pagkain o training, kapag ibinalik ito batay sa resibo sa pangalan ng employer at na-liquidate. Idinadagdag ito sa net pay at walang buwis. May buwis ang nakapirming allowance na walang resibo maliban kung de minimis.

## 中文

**给员工的报销要缴税吗？**

员工为公司垫付的费用（如燃油、餐费或培训），如果凭以雇主名义开具的收据报销并已核销，不属于工资，加到实发工资中，不计税。无收据的固定津贴需计税，属于小额免税福利的除外。

Source: NIRC Sec. 32(A) and RR 2-98, Sec. 2.78.1 (expenses incurred for the employer, liquidated with receipts, are not compensation)
