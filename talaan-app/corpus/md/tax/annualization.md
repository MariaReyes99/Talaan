# What is year-end annualization?

## English

**What is year-end annualization?**

In the last payroll of the year, the employer recomputes each employee’s tax on the whole year’s pay. If too much was withheld, the excess is refunded; if too little, the shortfall is deducted, by January 25 of the next year at the latest.

## Tagalog

**Ano ang year-end annualization?**

Sa huling payroll ng taon, kinukuwenta ulit ng employer ang tax ng bawat empleyado batay sa buong taon. Kung sobra ang nakaltas, ibinabalik ito; kung kulang, kinakaltas ang kulang, hindi lalampas sa January 25 ng susunod na taon.

## 中文

**什么是年终税款汇算？**

在年度最后一次发薪时，雇主按全年工资重新计算每位员工的税款。多扣的退还，少扣的补扣，最迟在次年 1 月 25 日前完成。

Source: BIR Revenue Regulations No. 2-98, as amended by RR 11-2018 (year-end adjustment)
