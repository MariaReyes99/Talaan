# Is converting unused leave to cash taxable?

## English

**Is converting unused leave to cash taxable?**

Unused service incentive leave must be paid in cash at year end or on separation. For private employees, cash for up to 10 days of unused vacation leave a year is a tax-free de minimis benefit; anything above that is taxable pay. Talaan applies the 10-day limit in final pay.

## Tagalog

**May buwis ba ang hindi nagamit na leave na ginawang pera?**

Dapat bayaran ng pera ang hindi nagamit na service incentive leave sa katapusan ng taon o sa paghihiwalay. Sa pribadong empleyado, walang buwis ang pera para sa hanggang 10 araw na hindi nagamit na vacation leave kada taon (de minimis); may buwis ang lampas doon. Inilalapat ng Talaan ang 10-araw na limit sa final pay.

## 中文

**未休假期折现要缴税吗？**

未休的服务奖励假须在年底或离职时折现支付。私营企业员工每年最多 10 天未休年假的折现属于小额免税福利，超出部分为应税工资。Talaan 在离职结算中套用 10 天限额。

Source: Labor Code Art. 95 (service incentive leave commutable to cash); RR 11-2018 (de minimis: monetized unused vacation leave up to 10 days)
