# Do minimum wage earners pay income tax?

## English

**Do minimum wage earners pay income tax?**

No. Minimum wage earners pay no income tax on their statutory minimum wage, holiday pay, overtime, night differential and hazard pay. Other taxable income they receive is still taxed.

## Tagalog

**May income tax ba ang minimum wage earner?**

Wala. Walang income tax ang minimum wage earner sa minimum wage, holiday pay, overtime, night differential at hazard pay. May tax pa rin ang ibang taxable na kita nila.

## 中文

**最低工资收入者要缴个人所得税吗？**

不用。最低工资收入者的法定最低工资、节假日工资、加班费、夜班津贴和危险津贴免缴个人所得税，但其他应税收入仍需缴税。

Source: NIRC Sec. 24(A)(2), as amended by RA 9504
